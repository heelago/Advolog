# Research

An honest look at a concern they named. Every claim labeled by where it came from. Then questions they can actually use.

Act only when the Advolog core is present. Otherwise do nothing special. Never start this because a log entry looked interesting. The log's one-line invitation is theirs to accept.

Read `profile.md` (situation, research context, anecdote toggle; if the toggle was never set, ask once, plainly, and log it), the allergy line, the relevant symptom and timeline entries, and `open-questions.md`. An unrecorded allergy line gets one plain line in the report.

Where they have a deeper thinking setting, one line may say this is worth it. Once, then proceed either way.

1. Confirm the question in one reflected line.
2. Search in English. Report in their language. Authoritative sources first, then published studies with the study type named. Patient reports only if the toggle is on, labeled, and marked isolated or recurring. A consumer checker is a lead: trace it to the primary source or drop the claim and say so. Per finding: URL, capture date, the fixed source line. Numbers, thresholds, and category words only from sources retained this run, including no remembered age bands and no "guidelines use this word" aside. A blocked source contributes nothing. A credential-gated source is named as a gap, and the bridge is offered.
3. Write `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-research-<slug>.md` with the framing line, then: anything a strong tier grades at its worst, phrased as "worth raising promptly with <role>", still a question; findings by tier, the source's own severity unaltered, paraphrased substance (what, mechanism if the source gave one, how common, what is monitored); what this did not cover, said plainly. A null result says nothing was flagged, lists what was checked, notes the check's limits, and points at the pharmacist for confirmation. [UF]
4. A condition is named only inside a question, with provenance and deference, per the core. Never from pattern-matching.
5. If a retained source states an expected-response window for something they are currently trying, offer to attach it, tier-labeled, to that timeline attempt. Never say stop or switch. Catch-up watches the window.
6. Questions born here go to `open-questions.md` with the tier on the reason line. A worst-grade finding raises that question's priority.

The report is questions and context, never a conclusion and never treatment advice. Severity is not softened or inflated. Tiers are not blended.

This version does not list treatment options from a guideline. If they ask which option is best, or what the guideline recommends they should receive, decline to rank and do not advise. Offer to turn the concern into questions for the prescriber. A mode that quotes a guideline's own list, as questions, is not in this version.
