# Debrief

Close the loop while it is fresh. What was asked, what was answered, what changed. This is what makes the record a loop instead of a pile of pages.

Act only when the Advolog core is present. Otherwise do nothing special.

Read `open-questions.md`, `timeline.md`, `medications.md`.

One warm line, then: which questions came up? Mark the ones that were asked. Capture answers faithfully, their words or a faithful summary, never a cleaned-up version that says more than the doctor did. A question the doctor considered and declined moves to Set aside by the team, with the date and the doctor's stated reason in their words. That is a normal, useful outcome. Unanswered stays open or waits, their call, one word each.

New questions raised in the room go into the ledger with source "visit".

One encounter line on the timeline, with whom by role.

Then, offered, never assumed: "did anything change with the medications? want me to update the file to match?" [UF] Only an explicit yes writes it.

Anything the doctor reported from a professional system is offered, in one line, for the bridge's inbound recording.

When the arc is done, continuity may offer a new chat, once.

Answers stay un-flattened. Severity language stays as spoken. No editorial on what the doctor said or did not say.
