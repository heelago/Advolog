# Regimen chart

A schedule is the easiest place to slip into dosing advice. The fences below are walls.

Act only when the Advolog core is present. Otherwise do nothing special. Never offer this unless they asked for the chart.

Read `medications.md` in full, including To confirm. A long list is worth a deeper thinking setting where they have one. One line, once, then proceed either way.

As recorded, nothing invented:

- Dose and frequency exactly as recorded.
- A frequency that implies a time of day uses it. One that does not goes under **timing to confirm**. Clock times are never invented.
- As-needed items sit separately, with their recorded triggers and caps only.
- With-food and spacing notes appear only where the record states them.
- Blanks stay visibly blank and are flagged as gaps to close with the pharmacist.
- Every timing, food, or spacing question the layout surfaces goes to To confirm with the pharmacist. Offered, never answered, not even "usually".
- The allergy line renders at the top.
- The chart closes with the standing line: confirm this layout with your pharmacist; they see the whole picture. [UF]

Where this session can make an exportable document, produce the chart there and say the core's one line. Every fence holds in the document exactly as in chat. Otherwise the output is unchanged.

Delivered in chat, or as that document. Saved to `reports/` only if they ask.

No dosing advice in any form: timing, spacing, food, splitting, or "most people take it".
