# Prep

One page a doctor can read in two minutes. What changed, what was tried, what to ask. Two speeds, one artifact. The fast speed does not pretend to have researched anything.

Act only when the Advolog core is present. Otherwise do nothing special.

## Full page

A visit exists, they ask to get ready, or a close offers it because the picture changed and a visit is coming.

Read `profile.md`, `medications.md`, `open-questions.md`, `symptoms.md` and `timeline.md` since the last encounter, and `professional-review.md`. At most a couple of questions: which visit, and anything that must not go unsaid.

This reads the whole record. Where they have a deeper thinking setting, one line may say it is worth switching on. Once, then proceed either way.

Build `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-prep-<visit>.md` from the skeleton in `references/report-templates.md`. The allergy line always renders. «אלרגיות: לא תועד» is visible, never omitted. Questions come from the ledger. A stale ledger gets a questions pass first. Patterns are observations, not interpretations. Every clinical fact traces to a record entry.

A thin record is still a real page: the allergy line and one good question already work. If the context is urgent, the after-hours line is pinned at the top.

Rehearsal, when that is the intent or they ask: add "what they are likely to ask you", shared with the questions skill.

The sheet is save-once. Asked-status updates happen at debrief, not now.

Run the pre-share checklist before delivery. Where this session can make an exportable document, produce the sheet there and say the core's one line about privacy and the shared link. The `reports/` copy stays the record.

## Express, when the visit is soon

A visit within days and nothing ready, "appointment tomorrow", or onboarding hitting an imminent visit. Ten minutes, from whatever exists, including nothing. Runs with no files. No research: there is no time to verify honestly, so it does not pretend to.

At most five questions, skippable, and they may answer in a batch: which visit and when; the one thing that must not go unsaid; current medications, and when captured fresh the allergy question rides on that same question, never as its own; anything new; anything to bring.

Then the sheet, immediately. Framing line, name or alias, allergy line always, questions first with a reason line and an answer blank, no tier labels because nothing was researched, medications, what's new, notes. Two-minute degrade: visit plus the one thing that must not go unsaid is still a real structured page. If even that is missing, the floor is three plain questions they can ask: what is my problem, what do I need to do, why is it important. Those are questions, not advice.

Pre-share in its lightest form: one line, glance at the page before handing it over.

Close, one sentence, once: "want me to turn today's sheet into the start of your file, later, when there's time?" [UF] A yes lands in onboarding at that later moment, not now. Nothing else is offered.

Speed is when diagnosis-shaped phrasing slips. It does not slip here.
