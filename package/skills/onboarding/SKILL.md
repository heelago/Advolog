# Onboarding

The first sitting should cost almost nothing and still land somewhere useful. One question at a time. Every slot can be skipped, and skipping never blocks the next step.

Act only when the Advolog core is present. Otherwise do nothing special.

Read `profile.md` if it exists. Never re-ask what is already there.

Ride the first message, as the core says. If they already told the story, confirm it in at most four lines and ask only about real gaps. If they pasted a filled opening form, treat it as the answers and go to the reflection. If they are in distress, offer to just hold things. Every remaining slot, including who this is for, may wait.

Slots, one at a time, skippable. Say that skipping is fine about every third question.

- Who this is for. Self, or someone else. The caregiver framing can itself be skipped. It gates only what leaves the record, not what is kept.
- The shape of the situation, inferred if you can: still looking, managing something known, or can't organize right now. Confirm the inference. Don't quiz.
- What would help, inferred, never as jargon: clarity before a visit, arriving with answers ready, or an active look into something.
- Anything soon? An imminent visit switches to express prep now. The rest waits.
- Is there a doctor or nurse they trust personally, family or a close friend, someone in medicine they could actually ask? [UF]
- Guidance level, with a default they can accept in one word: most people start with suggestions. [UF]
- Language, taken from how they write. Don't re-ask.

Once, at the start of ask-me mode: they can answer in conversation, or fill a short form where the platform makes one, and hand it back as one paste or one file. Nothing more.

Once, skippable: the account-privacy walkthrough for their platform, naming the training opt-out where it exists. It returns once at the first export. The steps are in the setup guides.

The first time a document or photo is offered, the core's one line about cropping the ID number.

Reflect back in four lines or fewer [UF]: who it is for, the situation in their words, what happens next, what was settled. "Did I get that right?"

In the same breath, say how saving will work, in one plain sentence, and write `profile.md`. That is the only file this skill writes. The forks from this sitting go in together.

Then the first small win, never a menu. Still looking: write down what has been tried, in their words. Managing something known: the allergy line, the one safety detail worth asking early, then medications, paste or conversation. Can't organize: hold things, nothing to set up. Imminent visit: express prep.

Never asked here: age, sex, diagnosis names, a medication list as a requirement, ID numbers (and say why if offered), alias, the patient-reports toggle, automations. Each has a later moment.
