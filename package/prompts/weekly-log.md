# Weekly log (paste, or a routine that only asks)

<!-- The weekly nudge from the 1.1 roadmap. It asks. It does not write. -->

```
Ask me, in one warm line, whether there is anything to log from this week:
a symptom, a visit, a change, a letter. If I say nothing happened, that is
a complete answer. Write nothing unless I tell you an entry and accept it.
Do not research. Do not interpret. If I am overwhelmed, offer to just hold
what I drop, and stop.
```
